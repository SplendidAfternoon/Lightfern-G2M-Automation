# Scaile Baseline — Lightfern AI Search Visibility

**Date:** 2026-06-20  
**Baseline method:** Perplexity AI search (snapshot today)  
**Forward tracking:** Scaile dashboard — 5 phrases added, monitoring from today  
**Lightfern visible in any query?** **No** — clear inbound gap and opportunity

---

## Executive summary (for deck slide 5)

Lightfern does not appear in AI search results for any of our 5 target terms today. Generic email writers (WriteMail, Mailmeteor, QuillBot) dominate transactional queries. Mission-aligned **product-intent** queries (voice preservation, anti-generic writing) still show no clear category leader — whitespace Lightfern can own with content. Founder queries surface general AI stacks (Claude, ChatGPT) but not email-specific tools — room for a "founder email stack" positioning play.

**Inbound strategy:** Publish 3 content pieces targeting the whitespace terms, use Scaile to measure lift over 2 weeks.
